M3 evidence: platform baseline on the zone clusters¶

What the platform baseline on the two OSC zone clusters (zerotrust-a, zerotrust-b) shows at M3, how each item is reproduced, and where a run's record lands. Run records are not committed: the evidence pack writes under bundles/ (ignored by Git), and the records of the M3 runs are delivered with the milestone submission, identified by the run, the commit and the cluster's kube-system UID.

Everything here is signed with the interim key (docs/contracts/keys/interim-cosign.pub), not the client trust chain, until the client's signing key and registry are delivered.

Running the pack on a zone¶

After install.sh on the zone, with the administrator kubeconfig of that zone and the fixture list the admission-fixtures workflow publishes:

ADMIN_KUBECONFIG=<zone administrator> FIXTURES=fixtures.txt BIN=<dir with the pinned Gatekeeper chart> \
PROVIDER_IMAGE=ghcr.io/<owner>/facis-zero-trust-demonstrator/admission-provider@sha256:<digest> \
TRUST_REPOSITORY=ghcr.io/<owner>/facis-zero-trust-demonstrator \
TRUST_KEY=docs/contracts/keys/interim-cosign.pub \
OUTSIDE_NAMESPACES="kube-system ztd-mgmt ztd-data" CLUSTER_KIND=target \
scripts/admission/evidence-pack.sh zone-a

Verify: the script prints the path of bundles/admission/zone-a/index.md and exits 0; the index shows both parts passed, lists every fault case, and records the run, the commit, the cluster's kube-system UID and the provider image. The fault script restores the provider and Gatekeeper to their recorded replica counts, the trusted key and the webhook on any exit; a FAIL restore: line means the restore could not complete (for example the API server was unreachable) and an operator re-runs install.sh before anything else.

Not in this baseline¶

  • Admission enforcement on the zone's own data plane (ztd-data) and its configuration through the umbrella chart: follows the umbrella integration.
  • The secrets baseline (OpenBao) on the zones: OpenBao moves out of the umbrella chart into a release of its own, installed after the zone's identity components, so it has its workload identity before it starts; until then it stays off on the zones.
  • The client signing key and registry (Harbor): the interim key and ghcr are used until delivery.
