Decision ledger¶

Every architecture decision of the demonstrator, where it is recorded, its status, and the evidence behind it. A decision is settled only when its record is merged with status Accepted; a record in an open pull request, or a number allocated and not yet written, is listed as such and is not presented as settled. Status as of 8 October 2026.

Numbers are allocated in planning (Architecture decisions).
